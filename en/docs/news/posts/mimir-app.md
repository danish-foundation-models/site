# Try Mimir on your phone: the DFM Mimir app

Danish AI can now run directly on your own phone. With the new app **DFM Mimir**, anyone can chat with **Mimir v1.5** — a Danish-trained language model that runs entirely locally, free of charge, without an account, and without sending your conversations to foreign servers.

When we released the first version of [Mimir in August](/news/2026/08/14/mimir-1-release-note.html), many people asked whether they could try it themselves. Now they can.

> "Many have asked whether they could run Mimir on their own device easily and conveniently. Now they can. This means that ordinary Danes can use Danish, sovereign AI completely free of charge and without sending their data abroad." — Torben Blach, Programme Manager, Danish Foundation Models

## Smarter, but still very small

Mimir is still a model with just 1 billion parameters, but it has become considerably smarter. According to [EuroEval](https://euroeval.com), Mimir v1.5 now outperforms models 20 times its size on Danish, and performs on par with models four times its size on English.

Among other things, Mimir v1.5 has been further trained on 125,000 documents from the Danish Parliament (Folketinget), recently made available by the Danish National Archives and the Danish Agency for Digital Government. The model was trained by DFM researchers at the University of Southern Denmark using compute from [UCloud](https://cloud.sdu.dk).

> "You can compare it to a person who has read more books. The brain of the model has not grown, but it knows more and uses it better. It shows that it is not size that matters when we develop language models — it is what you do with it." — Peter Schneider-Kamp, Professor, University of Southern Denmark

## What the app can do

The app comes with Mimir v1.5 bundled, so you can start chatting straight away — ordinary chats work offline and are stored on your device. You can use it to write, rewrite, brainstorm and ask questions in Danish or English, for example to summarise a text, plan meals or write a story.

## Help us make the next version better

Mimir cannot do everything yet: for example, it does not search the web by default or act on your behalf, and you will likely run into other limitations. That is why you can report problems directly in the app — it helps us make the next version much better. We have not yet reached the limit of what small, local models can do.
